Our Mission

The Israeli Imaginary Space Agency (IISA) stands at the forefront of space exploration, combining Israel's world-renowned technological innovation with an ambitious vision for humanity's future among the stars.

Since our founding, we have been dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what's possible, developing cutting-edge technologies that not only advance space exploration but also benefit life on Earth.
